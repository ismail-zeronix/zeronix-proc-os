Most small and mid-sized UAE companies do not need a full-time IT department, but they do need someone who picks up the phone when the printer, the Wi-Fi or a laptop stops working. An **IT annual maintenance contract (AMC)** gives you that team for a fixed monthly or yearly fee.

## What the contract covers

- **Help desk.** One number, one email and WhatsApp for every IT problem your people have, logged as tickets so nothing is forgotten.
- **Remote support.** Most issues are fixed in minutes over a secure remote session, without waiting for a visit.
- **On-site visits.** When something has to be touched, an engineer comes to your office. <!-- Owner: confirm visit areas and response time per emirate. -->
- **Preventive maintenance.** Regular checks of servers, backups, firewall, Wi-Fi and Windows updates, before small problems become downtime.
- **Users and devices.** New starters set up on day one, leavers' access removed the same day, laptops imaged and tracked in an asset list.
- **Microsoft 365 and email.** Accounts, mailboxes, shared drives, licences and security settings looked after for you.
- **Monthly report.** Tickets, devices, backups and risks in one page, so you can see what you pay for.

## How pricing works

Contracts are priced **per user** or **per device**, depending on how your office works. A 10-person office with a small server pays very differently from a 60-person firm with branch offices, so we start with a free visit and an inventory of what you have. <!-- Owner: add starting prices or "from AED x per user per month" if you publish them. -->

## Why companies switch to a managed IT contract

1. **Predictable cost.** A fixed fee instead of surprise call-out bills.
2. **Faster fixes.** Your systems are documented, so the engineer already knows your setup.
3. **Fewer problems.** Preventive work means fewer emergencies, not just faster repairs.
4. **One partner for devices and support.** The same team that supplies your laptops, network and CCTV keeps them running.

## Who it suits

Real estate brokerages, clinics, law firms, accounting practices, design studios, schools and trading companies across the UAE that have between 5 and 200 users and no in-house IT person, or one who needs back-up.
