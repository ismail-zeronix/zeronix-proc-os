Keys get copied, lost and never returned. An access control system replaces them with cards, PIN codes, fingerprints or face recognition, so you decide who can open which door and at what times, and you can see who came in. The same readers can record working hours for payroll.

## What we install

- **Door readers.** Card, PIN, fingerprint or face readers at entrances, server rooms, stores and offices. <!-- Owner: list the brands you supply (e.g. ZKTeco, Hikvision, Suprema). -->
- **Locks and door hardware.** Magnetic locks or electric strikes, exit buttons, door sensors and emergency release that opens doors in case of fire.
- **Controllers and software.** Central management of users, doors and schedules: add a new employee in a minute and block a leaver immediately.
- **Time attendance.** Clock-in and clock-out from the same readers, with daily and monthly reports of hours, late arrivals and absences that your HR or payroll can export.
- **Visitor access.** Temporary cards or codes for visitors and contractors that stop working at the end of the day.

## Why companies install it

1. **Security.** Only the right people enter sensitive areas, at the right times.
2. **Accountability.** Every door event is logged, and can be matched with [CCTV](/services/cctv-surveillance) footage.
3. **Payroll accuracy.** Real attendance data instead of paper sheets or estimates.
4. **No more lost keys.** A lost card is blocked in seconds; nothing needs to be rekeyed.

## How a project runs

We survey the doors, check the door frames and power, agree how each door should behave (always locked, open during working hours, open only for certain teams), and then quote a fixed price. Installation is done around your working hours, and we train the people who will manage users and reports.

## Part of a complete office setup

Access control works best planned together with the [network](/services/networking-wifi) and CCTV, especially in a new office. One team means one plan, one installation schedule and one number to call for support.

## Who it suits

Offices, clinics, schools, warehouses and residential buildings across the UAE, from a single front door to many doors across several floors.
