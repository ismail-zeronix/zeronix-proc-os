Opening or moving an office means dozens of IT decisions: internet lines, cabling, Wi-Fi, laptops, Microsoft 365, printers, phones, cameras, door access and meeting rooms. Each supplier handles one piece, and nobody owns the whole picture. We plan and deliver all of it as one project, so your team walks in on day one and everything works.

## What we plan and deliver

- **Internet and network.** Lines ordered in time, [cabling, switches, firewall and Wi-Fi](/services/networking-wifi) designed for the floor plan.
- **Devices.** Laptops, desktops, workstations and screens matched to each role, set up with your apps and ready to use. See our [products](/products).
- **Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.** Email, shared files, Teams and security set up, and data moved from your old systems.
- **Security.** Firewall, endpoint protection and backups from the start. See [cybersecurity](/services/cybersecurity).
- **Building systems.** [CCTV](/services/cctv-surveillance), [access control and attendance](/services/access-control) and [IP phones](/services/ip-telephony).
- **Meeting rooms.** [Screens, cameras and audio](/services/av-meeting-rooms) for one-touch video calls.
- **Printing.** Multifunction printers and scanning set up for each team.

## How we work

1. **Consultation.** We learn how your teams work, how many people you have now and in two years, and your budget.
2. **IT plan.** A single plan and timeline covering every item above, with clear prices and options where it makes sense to save.
3. **Coordination with the fit-out.** We work with your landlord and fit-out contractor so cables, power and ceiling points are right before walls close.
4. **Delivery and move.** Equipment installed and tested before move-in; for relocations we move your systems over a weekend to avoid downtime.
5. **Support.** After go-live, an [IT support contract](/services/it-amc-support) keeps everything running. <!-- Owner: confirm whether you offer project management for relocations and over which emirates. -->

## IT consultancy for existing offices

Not moving? We also review existing IT: what to keep, what to replace, where the risks are and how to bring costs down. You receive a clear, prioritised plan, not a sales pitch.

## Who it suits

Companies opening their first UAE office, growing teams moving to a bigger space, and businesses opening branches in other emirates.

Tell us your move date, office size and number of people, and we will prepare an IT plan and quote.
