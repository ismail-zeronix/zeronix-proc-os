A slow or patchy network costs time every day: calls drop, files take minutes to open and meeting rooms never connect on the first try. We plan, install and document office networks that simply work, from a small clinic to a multi-floor office.

## What we install

- **Structured cabling.** Cat6 or Cat6A to every desk, printer, access point and camera, tested, labelled and drawn on a floor plan.
- **Racks and patching.** A tidy rack with patch panels, power and space to grow, so changes take minutes instead of hours.
- **Switches.** Managed switches with PoE for access points, phones and cameras, set up with separate networks (VLANs) for staff, guests, phones and CCTV.
- **Firewall and internet.** A business firewall in front of your internet line, with secure remote access for staff who work from home. A second internet line can take over automatically when the first one fails.
- **Business Wi-Fi.** Ceiling access points placed after a site survey, one network name across the office, a separate guest Wi-Fi with its own password, and roaming between rooms without drops. <!-- Owner: list the Wi-Fi and switch brands you supply (e.g. Aruba, Ubiquiti, Cisco, TP-Link Omada). -->

## How a project runs

1. **Survey.** We visit, measure, check the ceiling and walls, and note where people, printers and screens will be.
2. **Design and quote.** You receive a plan with cable points, access points, equipment and a fixed price.
3. **Installation.** Work is scheduled around your hours, including evenings and weekends for occupied offices.
4. **Testing and handover.** Every cable is tested, Wi-Fi coverage is checked room by room, and you get the drawings, passwords and equipment list.
5. **Support.** Add the network to an [IT support contract](/services/it-amc-support) so changes and problems are handled for you.

## Moving or fitting out a new office?

Bring us in early, before the ceiling is closed. Cabling and access-point positions are much cheaper to get right during the fit-out than after it. We work with your fit-out contractor so the network, [CCTV](/services/cctv-surveillance), [access control](/services/access-control) and [meeting rooms](/services/av-meeting-rooms) are planned together.

## Signs your network needs work

- Wi-Fi drops in some rooms or when many people are connected.
- Video calls freeze while the internet line itself is fast.
- Nobody knows which cable goes where, or what the router password is.
- Guests use the same network as your accounts and servers.

Tell us your office size and the problems you see, and we will propose a fix and a price.
