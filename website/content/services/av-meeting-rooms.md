Every company knows the meeting that starts ten minutes late because nobody can connect the screen or the people on the call cannot hear the room. A well-planned meeting room makes video calls as easy as a phone call: walk in, touch one button, and talk.

## What we supply and install

- **Displays.** Commercial screens and interactive displays sized for the room and the distance to the furthest seat.
- **Cameras.** Conference cameras that frame the whole room or follow the person speaking.
- **Audio.** Speakerphones, ceiling or table microphones and speakers so everyone is heard clearly, without echo.
- **Room systems.** Microsoft Teams Rooms or Zoom Rooms kits with a touch panel on the table for one-touch join. <!-- Owner: list the brands you supply (e.g. Logitech, Yealink, Poly, Samsung, LG). -->
- **Wireless presentation.** Share a laptop screen without cables or adapters.
- **Room booking.** Panels outside the door that show if the room is free and let people book it.

## Rooms we design

- **Huddle rooms** for 2 to 4 people: an all-in-one video bar and a screen.
- **Meeting rooms** for 6 to 12 people: a camera with good framing, extra microphones and a larger screen.
- **Boardrooms** for 12 or more: dual screens, ceiling audio and a camera that tracks the speaker.
- **Training rooms**: projectors or large displays, presentation switching and good sound for the back of the room.

## How a project runs

1. **Room visit.** We look at the room size, the table, the lighting, the acoustics and where the cables can run.
2. **Proposal.** Equipment for each room with a fixed price, matched to the platform you use (Teams, Zoom or Google Meet).
3. **Installation.** Screens mounted, cables hidden, everything configured and tested with a real call.
4. **Training and support.** A short session for your staff, and support afterwards through an [IT support contract](/services/it-amc-support).

## Planning a new office?

Meeting room power, network points and ceiling microphones are easiest to add during the fit-out. Plan them together with the [network](/services/networking-wifi) so each room has the cables it needs.

Tell us how many rooms you have and their sizes, and we will propose a setup for each one.
