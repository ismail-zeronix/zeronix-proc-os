## What laptops for accountants must get right

- **Too little memory.** Large workbooks, Power Query and many browser tabs fill 8 GB quickly. 16 GB is the minimum; 32 GB for analysts and heavy models.
- **One small screen.** Reconciliations and reviews need two documents side by side. Two 24-inch monitors at the desk pay for themselves.
- **Slow storage.** A modern NVMe SSD opens and saves large files fast.
- **Cables and chargers.** A dock at each desk means one cable in, everything works.

## Month-end, VAT returns and EmaraTax

The busiest weeks for a UAE finance team are month-end close, quarterly VAT returns and corporate tax preparation, with filing through the FTA's EmaraTax portal. These are the weeks when a slow laptop costs real hours: large ledger exports, reconciliations with thousands of lines, and many browser tabs open across the bank portal, EmaraTax and the accounting system. Two things help most: enough memory, so nothing slows when everything is open, and two monitors, so source and working files sit side by side.

## Mac or Windows for accountants?

Windows is the safer default. Desktop accounting software such as Tally and Sage runs only on Windows, and Excel for Windows has features finance teams rely on, such as Power Pivot and some add-ins, that the Mac version lacks. MacBooks work well for teams that use only cloud accounting (Zoho Books, Xero, QuickBooks Online) and Excel on the web, but check every add-in before you switch.

## How long should finance laptops last?

Plan for four years. That is why memory matters at purchase: a laptop with two memory slots, such as the ThinkPad T14 Gen 5, can go from 16 GB to 32 GB in year two for a small cost, while a laptop with soldered memory cannot. Ask for a three-year warranty on the part number so the laptops are covered for most of their life.

## Buying for a finance team: checklist

1. List each person's main tools (Excel models, ERP, Power BI, banking).
2. Choose 16 GB or 32 GB per role, not one size for all.
3. Order docks, two monitors and full-size keyboards at the same time.
4. Turn on BitLocker and multi-factor authentication before day one.
5. Ask for serial numbers on the VAT invoice for your fixed asset register.

## Security for financial data

Finance teams hold bank access, payroll and customer data. Insist on a TPM 2.0 chip with BitLocker turned on, individual sign-ins (never shared), and multi-factor authentication for banking and EmaraTax access. If laptops leave the office, add a privacy filter.
