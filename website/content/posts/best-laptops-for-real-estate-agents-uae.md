## What laptops for real estate agents must do

- **Last a working day.** Viewings, a launch and evening calls: aim for a laptop rated well past 8 hours of real use, with fast charging.
- **Stay light.** Under about 1.4 kg in the bag every day; around 1 kg for agents who are out most of the day.
- **Look good on video.** A 1080p (or better) camera and decent microphones for video viewings with overseas buyers.
- **Be readable in bright rooms.** Show units and terraces are bright; 400–500 nits beats a sharper but dimmer screen.
- **Keep client data safe.** Passports, Emirates IDs, tenancy contracts and Form A, B and F paperwork pass through the laptop. Fingerprint or face sign-in and disk encryption matter if it is lost.
- **Run the CRM and portals smoothly.** 16 GB of memory keeps the CRM, portals, WhatsApp Web, email and Teams open together.

Raw processor speed matters least: a current Intel Core Ultra 5 or Apple M-series chip is more than enough for CRM work.

## Video viewings: camera, microphone and connection

Overseas buyers increasingly view off-plan and ready units by video before they fly in. A 1080p or better camera, microphones that cut background noise, and Wi-Fi 6E or Wi-Fi 7 make a visible difference on a call from a show apartment. The MacBook Air's 12 MP Center Stage camera keeps the agent in frame while they walk; the X1 Carbon offers FHD and higher-resolution cameras with an IR sensor for face sign-in. For calls from a site with weak Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot works, but agents who do many video viewings benefit from a laptop with good Wi-Fi.

## Common mistakes when equipping agents

- **Buying 8 GB laptops.** The CRM, two portals, WhatsApp Web, email and Teams together need 16 GB.
- **Choosing by processor.** A faster chip does not help an agent; a lighter laptop and a longer battery do.
- **Gaming or consumer laptops.** They are heavy, short on battery, and lack business warranty and security.
- **Different models for every hire.** It makes support, chargers and replacements harder; pick two standard models.
- **No encryption.** Client passports and contracts on an unencrypted laptop are a risk if it is left in a taxi.

## Equipping a brokerage, not just one agent

Most agencies do best with **two standard models**: a light premium laptop for senior agents and managers, and a value model for new agents. It keeps support simple (one disk image per model, shared chargers) and makes replacements quick when an agent joins on a Monday.

Plan the office at the same time: a USB-C dock and a 24 or 27-inch monitor on hot desks lets agents plug in one cable when they come back from viewings. For admin and back-office desks, a small desktop such as the [ThinkCentre M70q Gen 5](/products/desktops/lenovo-thinkcentre-m70q-gen-5) behind the monitor keeps desks tidy.

## UAE buying notes

- **Keyboards:** decide Arabic/English or English-only per user before ordering.
- **Warranty:** ask what the warranty covers for the exact part number, and whether it works abroad if agents travel to overseas roadshows.
- **Invoices:** you should receive a UAE tax invoice with 5% VAT and serial numbers for your asset list.
- **Security:** turn on BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault (Mac) and fingerprint or face sign-in before the laptop leaves the office.
