Zeronix Tech helps UAE companies buy the right business laptops, desktops, workstations and servers for the work their people actually do. We start from your industry and your roles, not from a price list: an estate agent, an architect, a receptionist and a finance manager need very different machines.

## What we do

- **Advice before you buy.** We match machines to roles and software, and say plainly when a cheaper model is enough.
- **Business-grade only.** Business series from the major brands, with Middle East part numbers, Arabic/English keyboards and UAE warranty.
- **Corporate buying made simple.** Volume pricing, UAE tax invoices with 5% VAT and your TRN, serial numbers for your asset register, and delivery across the Emirates.
- **Set-up on request.** Machines delivered ready to use: named, imaged or registered for Windows Autopilot.

## Who we work with

Real estate brokerages, architecture and engineering firms, law firms, clinics, finance teams, schools, hotels, logistics companies and corporate offices across the UAE. Our [buying guides](/guides) share what we have learned equipping these teams.

## Part of Zeronix

Zeronix Tech is the business IT catalogue of Zeronix ([zeronix.ae](https://www.zeronix.ae)).
